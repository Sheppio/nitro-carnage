Changelog

v0.1.122

A quieter console, and statistics by choice

  • The browser console no longer warns about soft shadows at the start of every race. The shadows look the same.
  • Opening the game once with ?analytics=on sends race statistics from that device even with Do Not Track switched on.
v0.1.121

Checking the race statistics

  • With ?debug in the address, the race readout now says whether race statistics are being sent, and if not, why not.
  • ?analytics=debug writes the same to the browser console, batch by batch.
v0.1.120

Counting races, anonymously

  • The game now counts which modes, tracks and seeds get raced, and how many races a visit lasts, to help decide what to build next.
  • It's anonymous: no names, no accounts and no cookies. Turning on Do Not Track in your browser switches it off.
  • The welcome screen for new drivers says so.
v0.1.119

A welcome for new drivers

  • The first time you play, the game asks your name, then takes you to the Garage to pick your car.
  • Your name has moved from the main menu to Settings.
  • The Garage button has left the main menu: it's still on the track screen and in the lobby.
  • The lobby has a Settings button, so you can change your name without leaving the room.
v0.1.118

A Nitro Carnage icon in your browser tab

  • The game's tab now shows an orange NC badge instead of a blank page, so it's easy to find among your other tabs.
  • Adding the game to an iPhone or iPad home screen gives it the same badge.
v0.1.115

No more freeze when the hotlap ghost first appears

  • On a new track, the game no longer stalls for a moment at the start of the second lap, when your ghost first takes to the road.
  • The ghost is now made fully ready while the track loads, not just started.
v0.1.112

Hosting a room: you can step off Track of the day again

  • On the room's track screen, the ‹ › kind of track could get stuck on Track of the day with a pad, so the host had to race it. Stepping right went to an empty Favourites and jumped back. Stepping left went to Seeded, which still held today's date, so the room read it as Track of the day again.
  • An empty Favourites now stays put until you step past it, and Seeded brings back your own seed word.
  • The same empty-Favourites fix applies to the quick race's track screen.
v0.1.110

A changelog, linked from the main menu

  • This changelog: every change so far, newest first.
  • A Changelog link on the main menu, on the version's line, aligned right.
v0.1.109

Windmill sails near upright

  • Windmill sails now lean back about 11°, as a real windshaft does, instead of 54°.
v0.1.108

Photo mode: save to the gallery on a phone, no Save on consoles

  • On a phone, Save photo opens the share sheet, so the photo goes into your gallery rather than the browser's downloads.
  • Xbox and PlayStation browsers block downloads, so consoles no longer show Save photo.
v0.1.107

Bots time the train, missiles set off mines, two random boxes a row, Leave race asks

  • Bots time their run to the level crossing at their own pace and stop only if the train will really be in the way. Expert bots wait about a quarter less, and none get hit.
  • A missile that flies into an armed mine sets it off, and the car beyond is unhurt.
  • Each row of pickups is two boxes, each a random useful kind.
  • Leave race in the pause menu asks first, with Stay as the default. A hotlap asks too.
v0.1.106

Photo mode: click or tap to focus

  • Click or tap the scene to focus the depth of field on that exact spot. A ring marks it for a moment.
  • On car focus now works the same way, so a car at the edge of the frame is as sharp as one in the middle.
v0.1.105

Photo mode from the pause menu in offline races

  • The pause menu in offline races has Photo mode. The world stays paused while a free camera flies around by keys, mouse, pad or touch.
  • Depth of field (focus distance, focus on car, blur strength), Hide UI, and Save photo as a PNG.
v0.1.104

Experimental camera views

  • A new Experimental… page in Settings lets you choose the race camera: Overhead, Helicopter, Chase far and near, Bonnet, Bumper, Cockpit, or Custom.
  • In a race, C or D-pad up cycles through the views.
  • The chase cameras swing round smoothly, and sounds pan from the camera's point of view.
v0.1.102

An always-on throttle setting

  • With Always-on throttle on, the car is flat out unless you brake, reverse or use the handbrake. The touch pad drops its accelerator button.
  • The settings rows sit closer together, so the longer list still fits without scrolling.
v0.1.101

v0.1.100
